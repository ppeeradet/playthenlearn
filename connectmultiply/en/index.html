Connect Multiply

The five-in-a-row multiplication game
Designed by BamBam
2–4 playersAges 7+Times tables 1–12
Download the rules (PDF) Full rulebook + designer's notes Open in YouTube

Goal

Multiply the cards in your hand to match a number on the board, place a chip, and be the first to connect 5 in a row (any direction) – or collect 5 gems – to win!

Setup

  1. Place the board in the center.
  2. Each player takes the 20 chips of one color.
  3. Shuffle all cards, deal 5 to each player; the rest is the draw pile.
  4. Put the Gem Tokens where everyone can reach them.
  5. Whoever last solved a math problem goes first.

On your turn

1

Option 1 · Multiply

Multiply 2 or more cards from your hand and place a chip on the matching number, e.g. 3 × 4 = 12.

2

or Option 2 · Discard

Discard 1–5 cards from your hand.

3

End of turn · Refill

At the end of your turn, draw back up to 5 cards.

Gem spaces – place a chip on a gem space and take 1 gem.
FREE spaces – if one of your chips is next to it, it counts as one of your spaces.

Special cards (one per turn · 5 of each)

Any number 1–12A wild card for any number 1–12, used with number cards.
Kick off!!Remove another player's chip. If it was on a gem space, they return 1 gem.
Swap CardsSwap your whole hand with another player.
Clean your handChoose a player: they discard their hand and draw 5 new cards.

Family/Junior and Expert modes are in the full rulebook. Adjust the number of special cards to suit your players.
